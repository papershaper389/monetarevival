Feldcheck: inspection app prototype

Prototype with the core features of an inspection app. Installable as a PWA and usable offline.

  • Inspections from templates with pages, Yes/No/N/A, notes, photos, follow-up actions, live score, signature, report
  • Issues from free text and photos, with simulated AI pre-fill
  • Actions with types (PPE order, SAP notification) and custom fields
  • Sites and assets with open items per asset
Sample data is randomly generated and fictional. Regenerate it under More. Data stays in this browser only.
